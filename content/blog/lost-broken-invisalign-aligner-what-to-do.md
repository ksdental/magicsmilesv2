If you lose or damage an Invisalign aligner, contact your treating dental office rather than deciding on your own to skip ahead, go backward, or stop wearing trays. The correct response depends on how long you wore the current set, how your teeth are tracking, and whether another aligner fits safely.

## First: Protect Your Treatment Progress

Because [Invisalign clear aligners](/cosmetic-dentistry/invisalign/) are removable, a tray can be misplaced at a restaurant, school, work, or while traveling. The [American Association of Orthodontists](https://aaoinfo.org/treatments/aligners/) notes that aligners are worn for most of the day and changed according to a prescribed sequence.

If your current tray is missing or damaged, contact the office managing your [Invisalign treatment](/cosmetic-dentistry/invisalign/) for instructions as soon as practical.

## Step 1: Search the Most Likely Places

Check the aligner case, bathroom counter, backpack, purse, car, desk, lunch area, and any napkins or food containers used during the last meal. If the tray is found, inspect it before reinserting it. A tray that is cracked, visibly distorted, or contaminated may need professional guidance.

## Step 2: Do Not Reshape a Warped Tray With Heat

Hot water can change the shape of thermoplastic aligners. Do not boil, microwave, bend, glue, trim, or otherwise attempt to repair a damaged tray at home. A tray that no longer matches the prescribed shape may not deliver the intended forces.

## Step 3: Keep Previous and Next Sets Available

The next tray is not automatically the right answer. In some situations, the previous tray may be used temporarily. The treating dentist needs to consider where you are in the wear cycle and whether an alternative tray seats correctly.

### Do Not Force the Next Aligner

A new tray can feel snug, but an aligner that will not seat, repeatedly pops off, or causes unusually strong pain should not be forced. Tell the office which tray was lost, how many days it had been worn, and whether the previous or next tray is available.

## What If the Aligner Cracks but Still Fits?

A small defect and a major split are not the same problem. Sharp edges can irritate the gums or cheeks, and a structural break may change how the aligner distributes force. Ask the treating office whether it can be worn temporarily.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Common mistakes include leaving trays in napkins, carrying them loose in pockets, exposing them to hot water, letting pets reach them, and changing the sequence without guidance. The AAO comparison of [braces and clear aligners](https://aaoinfo.org/whats-trending/braces-vs-clear-aligners/) highlights that removability is convenient but makes safe storage and consistent wear especially important.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if I lose my Invisalign aligner?

Contact the dental office managing your Invisalign treatment for instructions. Depending on when the aligner was lost and how treatment is progressing, the dentist may recommend a specific tray or another next step.

### Can I wear my previous Invisalign aligner if I lose my current one?

Sometimes a previous aligner may be recommended temporarily, but patients should not automatically switch trays without guidance from their treating dental professional.

### Can I fix a cracked Invisalign aligner at home?

Do not use heat, glue, trimming, or other do-it-yourself methods to repair an aligner. A damaged tray may no longer fit or apply forces as intended. Ask your dental office whether the aligner should be replaced or whether another option is appropriate.

## When an In-Person Check May Be Needed

An evaluation may be appropriate when no available tray fits, an attachment has come off, the aligner is cutting the tissue, pain is unexpected or severe, or treatment has been interrupted long enough that tooth position may have changed.
