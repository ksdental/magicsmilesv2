## Exams, cleanings, and diagnostic care

Routine exams and professional cleanings help the team monitor teeth and gums and remove deposits that home care may miss. X-rays can add information that is not visible during a clinical exam, but they are not automatically needed at every visit. [ADA MouthHealthy guidance](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/x-rays) says the decision depends on factors such as current oral health, age, disease risk, signs, symptoms, and previous images.

## Fillings, crowns, and bridges

When a tooth is damaged or decayed, the amount and location of the problem influence the restorative choice. Magic Smile Dental lists tooth-colored fillings as well as crowns and bridges. A filling may restore a more limited defect, while a crown may be considered when a tooth needs broader coverage. Bridges are one option for replacing missing teeth. The correct choice requires an exam.

## Root canal treatment and emergency dentistry

A new toothache, broken tooth, swelling, or another urgent concern is different from a preventive visit. General dentistry can provide the first evaluation and determine the next step. Root canal treatment is also listed among the practice’s services for situations in which the dental pulp is affected and the tooth may be restorable.

## When a general dentist is the right first call

For many patients, the general dentist is a practical first contact when they are unsure where to start. That can include preventive care, a new symptom, a damaged filling or crown, a cosmetic concern, or questions about replacing a missing tooth.

Starting with an evaluation also reduces the risk of choosing the wrong solution. A chipped tooth, for example, may call for different treatment depending on how much tooth structure is affected and whether there are other findings. Online information can explain possibilities, but it cannot diagnose the individual tooth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I transfer dental X-rays from another dentist?

Often, yes. A new dental office may be able to use recent diagnostic images if they are appropriate for the current evaluation. The ADA notes that a new dentist may ask a previous dentist for copies rather than automatically taking new images. Whether more imaging is needed depends on current oral health, symptoms, risk, and the usefulness of the existing images.

### Do I need a referral to start with a general dentist?

Usually, patients can contact a general dental office directly for routine care or an evaluation. Referral requirements can vary with an insurance plan or a particular specialist’s office. If specialty input is appropriate after the exam, the dental team can explain the next step.

### Can a general dentist help if I am not sure whether my problem is urgent?

Yes. A general dental office can triage many concerns and advise whether prompt evaluation is appropriate. Severe swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, significant facial trauma, or symptoms affecting breathing or swallowing can require urgent medical attention rather than waiting for a routine dental appointment.

### Is cosmetic dentistry the same as general dentistry?

No. General dentistry centers on prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of common oral-health needs, while cosmetic dentistry focuses primarily on smile appearance. The areas can overlap, but the appropriate category and treatment depend on the clinical need and the patient’s goals.

### Can children and adults use the same general dental practice?

Some general dental practices care for a broad age range, while certain children or clinical situations may be referred to a pediatric dentist. Magic Smile Dental describes its general dentistry care as serving the entire family. Ask the office about age-specific care when scheduling for a child.
